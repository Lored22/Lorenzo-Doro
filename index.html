Good to know

A few things, before dinner.

Can I place a real order?

Not yet. This is a partner preview: products, packaging and prices are illustrative. The bag and checkout let you explore the experience without making a purchase.

What is inside each gift box?

Discovery includes truffle sauce and tagliatelle. Date Night adds honey, truffle salt and taralli. Signature also includes balsamic. Peperoni Cruschi are sold separately in this preview and are not included in the boxes.

Are delivery and imports confirmed?

No. Suppliers, Korean import requirements, final labels and delivery arrangements must be confirmed before a commercial launch.

What happens to my data?

Only your language and bag can be remembered in this browser. Delivery details stay in memory while you edit your selection, and are cleared when you leave or finish the shopping journey. No personal details are saved or sent by the demo forms.
