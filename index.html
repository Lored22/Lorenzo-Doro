Good to know

A few things, before dinner.

Can I place a real order?

Not yet. This is a partner preview: products, packaging and prices are illustrative. The bag and checkout let you explore the experience without making a purchase.

What is inside each gift box?

Discovery pairs truffle sauce and tagliatelle. Date Night adds honey, salt and taralli. Signature includes all six pantry pieces. Open a box to see its full selection.

Are delivery and imports confirmed?

No. Suppliers, Korean import requirements, final labels and delivery arrangements must be confirmed before a commercial launch.

What happens to my data?

The demo remembers only your language and bag in this browser when storage is available. Delivery details are used only to show the current checkout summary and are cleared when it closes or finishes. No email, address or payment data is saved or sent by the demo forms.
